How points work

Each closed issue is worth 1 point, split equally among its unique human commit authors across associated merged PRs.

  • Bot and unknown commit identities excluded
  • Bot, unmerged, cross-repository, and directly reverted PRs excluded
  • Labels, merge speed, and review volume do not affect rank
